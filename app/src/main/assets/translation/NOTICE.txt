Changes/usage in this application: existing quantized ONNX encoder/decoder
weights are packaged unchanged, verified against published LFS SHA-256 values.
The application uses its own Kotlin unigram tokenizer with NFKC normalization,
greedy decoding (not upstream beam search), and short-input/output limits.
Translation quality may differ from the original implementation. No endorsement
by the University of Helsinki, Xenova, or Microsoft is implied.

Runtime: Microsoft ONNX Runtime 1.23.2, MIT License.
https://github.com/microsoft/onnxruntime/tree/v1.23.2
See licenses/ONNX-Runtime-MIT.txt.

These models can produce incorrect or biased translations. Check suggestions
before saving. Translations are generated locally, without a network service.
